# Trading Bot Fee & Referral Management System

### System Overview & Purpose
The **Trading Bot Fee & Referral Management System** is a dedicated web application designed to track, bill, and manage monthly subscription fees for trading bot clients while managing and auditing referral commissions.

* **Client Bot Fee**: **\$50.00 / month** per client.
* **Regional Manager Referral Bonus**: **\$2.50 / client** earned by the Regional Manager (Talha) whenever a client pays their monthly bot fee.
* **Separation Principle**: The client’s \$50 bot fee and Talha’s \$2.50 referral bonus are completely decoupled. Collecting the client’s fee sets the commission to **Unpaid / Pending Release**, allowing administrators to review and disburse referral bonuses independently.

## 4. Key Features & Functionality

### A. Real-Time Statistics & All-Time Analytics Dashboard
The top analytics bar displays four metric cards:
1. **Total Bot Clients**: Total registered accounts managed under the bot system.
2. **Current Month Collection**: Number and percentage of paid clients for the selected month, along with total \$ collected (e.g., `50 paid (96%) | $2,500 collected`).
3. **All-Time Referral Bonus ($2.50)**: Aggregates total referral commissions earned by Talha across **all-time paid clients** across all months and years (e.g., `53 clients × $2.50 = $132.50`).
4. **All-Time Referral Payout**: Tracks how much referral money has been disbursed to Talha vs. what remains pending release (e.g., `$2.50 received | $130.00 pending release`).

### B. Interactive 12-Month Table
* **Client & Identity**: Displays client name and email.
* **Billing Cycle & Next Due**: Shows registration date (`1st`) and next renewal date (`Next`), automatically calculated using calendar month rolling logic.
* **Referral Bonus ($2.50)**:
  * **When Fee is Paid**: Shows interactive status button:
    * `[ ⏳ Bonus Pending (Click to Pay) ]` (Pulsing Amber) – Click to release bonus.
    * `[ ✓ Bonus Paid ($2.50) ]` (Emerald) – Bonus completed.
  * **When Fee is Unpaid**: Displays `Bot Fee Unpaid` in muted gray.
* **Month Columns (Jan – Dec)**:
  * Clicking any month cell opens the **Bot Fee Payment modal** with options for `PAID ($50)`, `NEED TO PAY`, `UNSET`, custom payment date, and notes.
  * When in the **Bonus Pending** tab, clicking a paid month cell instantly marks the bonus as paid without touching the bot fee.

### C. Multi-Level Filtering
* **Quick Filter Tabs**:
  * **All Clients**: Shows all registered bot clients.
  * **Need to Pay ($50)**: Clients with due bot fees for the selected month.
  * **Paid ($50)**: Clients who have paid their $50 fee for the selected month.
  * **Bonus Received ($2.50)**: Clients whose referral commission has already been paid out to Talha.
  * **Bonus Pending ($2.50)**: Clients whose bot fee is paid, but Talha’s referral commission is awaiting payout.
  * **Unset**: Clients whose billing status has not yet been set for the month.
* **Instant Search**: Real-time filtering by client name, email.


### E. Reminders & Client Communications
* **Unpaid Client Reminders Modal**: Lists all clients who have not yet paid the current month’s $50 bot fee.
* **Copy WhatsApp / Email Template**: Generates formatted, ready-to-send payment reminder messages with one click.

### F. Excel Export
* Downloads a complete Excel spreadsheet (`Bot_Fee_Records_<Year>.xlsx`) formatted with client contact information, monthly fee history, payment dates, and referral payout statuses.

## 5. Backend REST API Endpoints

All bot endpoints are secured via JWT/HMAC bearer token authentication (`requireAuth`):

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `POST` | `/api/login` | Authenticates users (`HussainIjaz` or `Talha`) and returns session token. |
| `GET` | `/api/bot/records` | Retrieves bot clients with search, year filtering, and next-due calculation. |
| `POST` | `/api/bot/records` | Creates a new bot client ($50 monthly fee, $2.50 referral rate). |
| `PUT` | `/api/bot/records/:id` | Updates client profile (name, email, dates, notes). |
| `DELETE` | `/api/bot/records/:id` | Deletes a client from `bot_records` (leaves VPS records untouched). |
| `PUT` | `/api/bot/records/:id/payment` | Updates month payment (Paid/Due/Unset). Automatically sets bonus to `pending`. |
| `PUT` | `/api/bot/records/:id/referral-status` | 1-click endpoint to toggle referral status (`received` vs `pending`). |
| `POST` | `/api/bot/records/batch-payment` | Bulk updates payment statuses for selected client IDs. |
| `POST` | `/api/bot/records/batch-referral-status` | Bulk updates referral commission status for selected client IDs. |
| `GET` | `/api/bot/stats` | Returns current month stats and all-time referral metrics. |
| `GET` | `/api/bot/export-excel` | Generates and streams downloadable Excel workbook. |
